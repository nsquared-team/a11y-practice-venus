Demo 2: Label Association WCAG 1.3.1 — Level A

A label can look right visually but not be connected to its field programmatically. Try the click test: click on the label text. Does the input get focus?

Broken

Email address Phone number

These look like labels but are just <span> elements. Clicking "Email address" does nothing. A screen reader announces "edit text" with no context.

Fixed

Click the label — the input gets focus. The for attribute connects each label to its field. A screen reader announces "Email address, edit text."
