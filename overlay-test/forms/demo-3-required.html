Demo 3: Required Fields WCAG 1.3.1 — Level A

How do you communicate that a field is required? A red asterisk alone isn't enough.

Broken

The asterisk is visual-only. A screen reader may announce "star Full name" or may skip it entirely. There's no programmatic indication that these fields are required.

Fixed

The required attribute tells screen readers "required" when the field gets focus. The visible "(required)" text is clear to everyone without relying on an asterisk convention.
