About Discover Venus

The solar system's premier Venus travel agency

Our Story

From a bold dream to interplanetary reality, Discover Venus has been pioneering luxury space tourism since 2035.

2035

The Beginning

Discover Venus was founded by a team of former astronauts and luxury travel experts with a shared vision: making Venus accessible to adventurous travelers.

2038

First Atmospheric Tours

We launched our first cloud-deck observation tours, allowing guests to experience Venus's stunning golden atmosphere from specially designed observation vessels.

2042

Surface Expeditions Begin

With breakthrough thermal protection technology, we became the first company to offer guided surface expeditions to Venus's volcanic highlands.

2045

10,000 Travelers Milestone

We celebrated our 10,000th traveler with a perfect safety record, cementing our reputation as the most trusted name in Venus tourism.

Today

Leading the Way

With over 50 unique destinations and three distinct package tiers, we continue to expand what's possible in interplanetary travel.

Our Mission

Safety First

Your safety is our absolute priority. Every aspect of our operations is designed with multiple redundancies and rigorous protocols.

Our Safety Protocols

  • Triple-redundant life support systems on all vessels and surface vehicles
  • 24/7 mission control monitoring from our Earth-based operations center
  • Mandatory safety training for all travelers before departure
  • Real-time health monitoring throughout your journey
  • Emergency evacuation capabilities at all surface destinations
  • Comprehensive medical facilities staffed by space medicine specialists

Certifications & Compliance

  • International Space Tourism Safety Board (ISTSB) Certified
  • United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA) Licensed
  • Interplanetary Tourism Association Gold Standard
  • ISO 45001:2050 Occupational Health & Safety Certified
