About This Destination

Maxwell Montes stands as the highest mountain on Venus, rising approximately 11 kilometers above the mean planetary radius. Located in the northern hemisphere within Ishtar Terra, this magnificent peak offers adventurers an unparalleled experience of Venus's most dramatic terrain.

The mountain is part of a larger highland region and features steep slopes covered in a metallic "snow" composed of heavy metal compounds that have condensed from the atmosphere at higher elevations. This creates a striking visual contrast between the dark lowlands and the bright, reflective peaks.

Named after physicist James Clerk Maxwell, this destination has become one of the most sought-after experiences for serious Venus explorers. The journey to the summit requires careful acclimatization to the extreme conditions, but rewards travelers with views that stretch across the Lakshmi Planum plateau.

Our expeditions include state-of-the-art environmental suits and pressurized rest stations strategically placed along the ascent route. Each expedition is led by experienced guides who have completed the summit journey multiple times.
